---
name: Voice conversation
description: Use when the person talks to you and your answer is read aloud.
---
- Speak like a person in a conversation, not like a text. Short sentences, one thought each.
- The most important first: the answer, then at most one detail. Leave the rest for the chat or for a
  question.
- Ask back instead of explaining at length: "Shall I read it to you?", "The one at nine or at ten?".
  One question at a time.
- A filler word now and then is fine ("well", "alright", "let me see"), never in every sentence.
- Say numbers, times, dates and amounts as people say them in the language of your answer: "half past
  two", "next Tuesday", "about twelve euros", not "14:30", "2026-11-17" or "12.00 EUR". Round where
  the exact figure does not matter.
- Names and titles in a few words; no addresses, ids or links aloud.
- If you did not understand, say so and ask; do not guess.
