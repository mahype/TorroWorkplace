# Chat

**Chat** is built on Nextcloud Talk and kept simple: one contact list window and one small window per conversation, opened with a double-click. It needs a Nextcloud account (see the topic "accounts").

## The contact list

- A switch between "People" and "Groups", and "New chat".
- Everyone on your Nextcloud with their status (online, away, busy, do not disturb, offline) and status message; people online are on top. Whom you can see depends on the sharing settings of your Nextcloud.
- Your own status at the bottom, with "Set status message…". It is also in the tray menu.

## Being shown online

Torro Workplace › "General" › "Chat" › "Show others when I am online" is off at first. While it is off the list says "You appear offline to others". When it is on, the suite tells Nextcloud every few minutes whether you are online or away, from whether you use the computer, and sends nothing while the screen is locked.

"Ring for incoming calls" is in the same place.

## Good to know

- Unread chats also show in the Notification Hub, where you can reply quickly.
- Files, pictures and voice messages can be opened or played; recording voice messages is not there yet.
- The history of a chat is kept only while the app runs. Without a connection nothing is sent later.
- AI agents never make or take calls.
