# Assistant

The **Assistant** is an AI chat that can work in the suite: read your calendar, find mail, make tasks, and more, with the rights you give it. Its settings have "General", "Access", "Profile", "Bots", "Tools", "Voice", "History" and "API and MCP".

## Models

The assistant answers with a model of an AI account (see the topic "accounts"). Models on this computer (Ollama and others) come first, then OpenRouter, then the other providers. You choose the account and the model per conversation; only models that can use tools are offered. Models are switched on and off in Torro Workplace › "AI", or with "Manage models…" in "General".

## What it may use

"Access" sets per area what the assistant may do ("None", "Read", "Write" or "Full"), and which mail accounts, calendars and lists. Changes count from its next step. In a conversation you can switch areas off for it, never on beyond its access.

Deleting and sending wait for your approval: a card in the chat with "Allow" and "Deny", also in the tray, the Notification Hub and as a notification. Voice cannot approve.

The built-in tools (files, PDF, the web) are set up in Torro Workplace › "MCP servers" › "Built-in tools". Which of them, which MCP servers and which bots the assistant may use is set in its settings › "API and MCP" › "Used by the assistant". Web search runs through the providers you switch on (SearXNG, Brave, Tavily, Exa, Mojeek, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia); you confirm it once per conversation.

## Profile and memory

"Profile", then the row with the assistant's name: its name, description, picture and personality ("Calm and brief", "Friendly", "Formal" are starting points). The personality changes its tone, never its rights. Under "About you" you can give your own name; the assistant then calls you by it. Leave it empty if it should not know it.

"Profile" › "Memory": notes the assistant keeps because you asked it to ("Remember that …"). You can read, change and forget them. After it read data from outside, such as a mail, each new note waits for your approval.

## Bots

Bots are assistants for one job, with their own profile, model and rights. The assistant asks a bot only when you name it. Bots cannot ask other bots.

## Voice

"Voice" is off at first: a wake word ("Hey Torro"), speech to text and speech output, all on this computer. The tray shows when the microphone listens.

## Help

The assistant can read this help. Ask it how something works in the suite.
