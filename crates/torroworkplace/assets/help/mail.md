# Mail

Mail is one of the three windows of the inbox app. It shows all your mail accounts together.

## The window

The sidebar holds "New email", "All", the categories, "Reply later", "Set aside", "Follow up" and the folders ("Drafts" and the others of each account). The window that writes a mail saves drafts by itself.

## Several accounts

Add each account in Torro Workplace › "Accounts" › "Add mail account…" (see the topic "accounts"). In the Mail settings (its gear) › "Accounts", each account has the tabs "Overview", "Settings", "Storage & search", "Folders", "Sync" and "Backup".

The other sections of the Mail settings: "Reading & writing", "Attachments", "Categorization", "Automation" and "Statistics".

## Filters

Filters are in "Categorization", or "Create filter…" on a mail or from the search field. A filter is a saved search (`from:`, `subject:`, `has:attachment` …) for one account or "All accounts". It can set a category, mark as read or move to the trash; it never deletes for good. At most 200 filters.

## Undo

Ctrl + Z (⌘ Z on macOS) or `u` undoes the last archive, delete, move or snooze, within 5 seconds. "Delete permanently" and "Unsubscribe" ask first and cannot be undone. Sending cannot be undone.

## Attachments

"Save as…", the Downloads folder, or a save target: a folder with a naming pattern and rules for when it is suggested ("Manage save targets…" in an attachment's menu). Files are never overwritten ("Keep both" or "Skip if identical"). At most 50 MiB per file. "Save as EML" saves the original mail unchanged.

## Automation

"Automation" holds workflows, rules and runs, for example filing invoice PDFs from mail. There are templates for PDFs attached directly and for Stripe and Paddle/Setapp invoices. A test run really saves a file. Rules run only while they are on; a rule switched on catches up at most 7 days of past mail.

## Backup

Per account › "Backup": back up a whole account or the mail from a start date, and restore it.
