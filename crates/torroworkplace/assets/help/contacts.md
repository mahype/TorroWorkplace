# Contacts

**Contacts** shows your address books over CardDAV: Nextcloud first, also iCloud, Fastmail, mailbox.org and Radicale. Google and Microsoft address books are not supported.

Without an account the app says "Connect an account in Torro Workplace": add a Nextcloud account or a mail account with CardDAV in Torro Workplace › "Accounts" (see the topic "accounts").

## The app

- "New contact", "All contacts", "Favorites", "Groups", "Birthdays" and "Address books".
- On a contact: "Add to favorites", "Add to group", "Call" and "Copy mail address".
- Searching works as you type, over name, company, mail, phone, city and notes, ignoring case and accents.
- Several address books and accounts show in one list.
- You can read and change contacts without a connection. When a contact was changed elsewhere at the same time, the app shows the conflict and never overwrites it silently.

The assistant can look up contacts only in the address books you give it in its access.

Contacts has no entry in the tray menu; open it from the app launcher.
