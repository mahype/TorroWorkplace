Visual review · direction B

A quiet board for the things you need nearby.

Pinboard is a glanceable surface beside the work: notes, checklists, screenshots, images, links and files, placed freely on one open board. The dots are a gentle alignment guide, not cells. Color helps scanning; pictures and documents get room to be seen.

Window sizes

The same board adapts to its tile. Pins stay freely placed on the open canvas; the dotted 8 px guide helps align them without forming a card grid. Wide keeps board navigation visible, medium folds it to a 60 px rail, and narrow becomes a readable list without dragging.
